About Our School

Tshovani Primary School has been part of the Chiredzi community for over three decades. We blend solid academics with sport, culture, and care — because a child who is happy, safe, and curious learns best.

  • Trained, dedicated teachers in every grade
  • Strong literacy and numeracy foundations
  • Safe, friendly grounds with space to play
  • Feeder relationships with local secondary schools

Our Programs

Every child finds something to love — and something to be great at.

Early Years

Play-based learning that sparks curiosity from day one, building confidence, language, and friendship.

Junior & Senior Primary

Core skills in reading, writing, mathematics, science, and heritage studies — taught to master, not just to pass.

Sports & Athletics

Football, netball, and athletics build teamwork, discipline, and healthy bodies — with inter-school fixtures.

Arts & Culture

Music, traditional dance, drama, and art celebrate our heritage and give every learner a stage.
